PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCREEN_CONTROL
	EN	Eversolo Screen Control

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCREEN_CONTROL_DESC
	EN	Controls the Eversolo DMP-A8 screen based on playback state for this player. When enabled, the screen turns ON when music starts playing and turns OFF after a configurable delay when playback pauses or stops. Configure the settings below for each player individually.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_ENABLED
	EN	Enable Eversolo Screen Control

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_ENABLED_DESC
	EN	Tick to activate screen control for this player. Leave unticked for players that are not connected to an Eversolo.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_AUTO_IP
	EN	Auto-detect Eversolo IP

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_AUTO_IP_DESC
	EN	A last resort only, for a Squeezelite running on the Eversolo itself: use the player's own live IP as the device address. The address below, and any device found on the network, both take priority - so this can be left ticked and ignored.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP_DETECTED
	EN	Current player IP:

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP
	EN	Eversolo IP Address

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP_BRIDGED
	EN	This player is bridged or virtual, so it has no address of its own - which does not matter: the Eversolo is reached at its own address below, found by the network scan or entered by hand.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP_DESC
	EN	The address of the Eversolo this player feeds. Leave it empty to use the device found by the network scan; set it to pin one particular Eversolo, which always wins. Press Scan to look for Eversolos on the network.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCAN
	EN	Scan

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCANNING
	EN	Scanning the network for Eversolo devices - reload this page in a few seconds to see what it found.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCAN_FOUND
	EN	Eversolo devices found on the network:

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCAN_NONE
	EN	The last scan found no Eversolo answering the control API. Check the device is awake and on this subnet, then Scan again - or enter its address by hand.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP_INUSE
	EN	Commands are currently being sent to:

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_PORT
	EN	Eversolo API Port

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_PORT_DESC
	EN	HTTP control port (default: 9529). Only change if your device uses a non-standard port.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_DELAY
	EN	Screen Off Delay (seconds)

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_DELAY_DESC
	EN	Seconds to wait after pause/stop before turning the screen off (default: 30, range: 0–600).
