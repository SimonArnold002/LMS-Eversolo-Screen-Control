PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCREEN_CONTROL
	EN	Eversolo Screen Control

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_SCREEN_CONTROL_DESC
	EN	Controls the Eversolo DMP-A8 screen based on playback state for this player. When enabled, the screen turns ON when music starts playing and turns OFF after a configurable delay when playback pauses or stops. Configure the settings below for each player individually.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_ENABLED
	EN	Enable Eversolo Screen Control

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_ENABLED_DESC
	EN	Tick to activate screen control for this player. Leave unticked for players that are not connected to an Eversolo.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_TICK
	EN	Enabled

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_POWER
	EN	Control Eversolo power

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_POWER_DESC
	EN	Turn the Eversolo off and on with this player's power button. Powering OFF is sent over the network; powering ON is a Wake-on-LAN packet, which the Eversolo only accepts on its WIRED network port and with WOL enabled on the device. Leave this off unless this player feeds the Eversolo - a stray power-off costs a full cold boot.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_POWER_NOMAC
	EN	The device's hardware address is not known yet, so it cannot be woken. Open this page once while the Eversolo is switched ON and it will be learned automatically.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_HOME_POWER
	EN	Power button on Material's Home screen

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_HOME_POWER_DESC
	EN	Adds an Eversolo Power tile to Material's Home screen, with a power button for this player's Eversolo that still works once the device is off and its player has gone from LMS. Needs "Enable Eversolo Screen Control" and "Control Eversolo power" ticked too, and the Eversolo's address filled in above - without an address there is nothing for the tile to open, so it is not offered. Refresh Material after saving for the tile to appear. Unticking stops the tile being offered; one already on your Home screen stays until you unpin it.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_PP_ERROR

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP
	EN	Eversolo IP Address

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_IP_DESC
	EN	The address of the Eversolo this player drives. Find it on the device: Settings > About. Give it a fixed address on your router so it cannot change. The name of the device appears here once it has been asked - the player's own address is irrelevant, so a player fed through a bridge works exactly the same way.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_PORT
	EN	Eversolo API Port

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_PORT_DESC
	EN	HTTP control port (default: 9529). Only change if your device uses a non-standard port.

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_DELAY
	EN	Screen Off Delay (seconds)

PLUGIN_EVERSOLO_DELAY_DESC
	EN	Seconds to wait after pause/stop before turning the screen off (default: 30, range: 0–600).
